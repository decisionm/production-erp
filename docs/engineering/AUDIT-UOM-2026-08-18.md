# Unit of measure — evidence audit and the canonical contract (18 Aug 2026)

Commissioned after the owner corrected a dangerous assumption: **production materials are not
all kilograms.**

## The evidence, and its limits

Source: the 12-Aug Tally export set (UTF-16LE XML), read as evidence only — never copied into
this repo (Q38 remains open). Rates, amounts, party names and GSTINs were excluded by tag
name and appear nowhere below.

**What it is:** 382 purchase-order line items across 43 stock items, plus a pending-order
report. **What it is NOT:** there is no GRN and no Stock Journal in this set. So PO-side units
are **proven**; the claim "the PO unit is also the stock-keeping unit and the unit that posts
to Tally" is **suspected, not proven** — and that is precisely the claim a UOM contract rests
on. *A Stock Journal / GRN export has been requested.*

Extraction validated independently: it yields 28 dual-unit lines of 382, and Q40 records "28
of 382" from a separate earlier count.

## What the factory actually measures in

**Three unit strings exist. Nothing else.** `Kgs.` · `Nos.` · `Pcs.` — all with a trailing
dot, because `items.uom` carries Tally's `BASEUNITS` verbatim.

| | items | examples |
|---|---|---|
| **`Kgs.`** | 17 | Relpet, Pet Resin, HDPE Blow Material, every masterbatch — **and six packing-FILM masters** |
| **`Nos.`** | 26 | trays, master boxes, caps, measuring cups, PAD, **packing tape** |
| `Pcs.` | 0 as a base unit | appears only as one item's alternate |

**26 of 43 items are counted, not weighed.** Any code treating "material" as "kilogram" is
wrong for most of the catalogue.

**There is no `Roll`, `Mtr` or `Box` unit anywhere.** Tape is `Nos.` — "Box" appears 196 times
but only inside item *names*. FC-03 and DEC-20260807-005 already settle tape: one No = one
roll = 65 metres, display-only.

### Fractions are sharply bounded — which makes the COUNT rule safe

- **Not one fractional `Nos.` or `Pcs.` exists**, across 1,045 observed quantities.
- **All 15** fractional values in the entire set are **packing film in `Kgs.`** (e.g. 14.700).
- Resin and masterbatch show whole kg *in this evidence* — absence of evidence, not evidence
  of absence; decimals stay permitted for weight.

Precision is not a defect: every quantity column is already `decimal(_,4)`. **The missing
thing was the unit, not the scale.**

## The canonical contract

`items.uom` is Tally's `BASEUNITS`, verbatim, and is the single source of truth. On top of it,
one classifier — `MeasurementType::forUom()`:

| Measurement type | Fractions | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `Weight` | permitted | kg family |
| `Count` | **refused** | Nos./Pcs. family |
| `Unknown` | permitted | **never collapses to weight** — refusing decimals on an unclassified unit would block real work on a guess |

It replaces four divergent predicates that were asking one question three ways
(`Item::KG_UOM_VARIANTS` enumerated dotted spellings; `GoodsReceiptService::isMassUom` stripped
dots and added "kilogram(s)"; the frontend held a fourth copy). Against Tally's actual `Kgs.`
all of them agreed — **drift caught before it bit, not a live defect.**

**It classifies the UNIT, never the MATERIAL.** Packing film is measured in kilograms and is
not resin. Whether a given kg material is a "common input" that may not name a machine is
**Q54(a)** and belongs to the factory.

## Enforced

- A **counted** material refuses a fractional quantity on both the material request and the
  store issue, naming the item's own unit in the refusal.
- A **weight** material keeps its decimals.
- An issue may **not** name a unit the item does not use. The request side already refused
  this on FC-03 grounds; the issue side accepted *and persisted* a caller-supplied unit.
- `TwoUnitsChainTest` walks PO → GRN → RM Store → Request → Issue → Production/WIP twice —
  once in `Kgs.` with a decimal quantity, once in `Nos.` with whole trays — and asserts the
  floor panel reports each item's **own** unit.

## PR #196 itself: cleaner than main, not worse

It introduces **no new kg assumption**. `quantity_kg` on bag scans, `bag_weight_kg` and the
"Kg/bag" label are all pre-existing on main and legitimately weight-only (the bag table is
bag-only by construction; the form is server-gated). What is new is either correctly gated
(`weightPerBag`, rendered only under a mass-unit check) or genuinely UOM-driven: the
Production/WIP panel carries `item.uom` end to end, and `formatQuantity()` appends the item's
own unit **with no kg fallback**. It is the first change in this codebase to carry a unit
through the chain.

## The guard that was written and could not fire

**Correction to the section above, found by the re-gate and worth stating plainly:** when
first written, the fraction and unit guards sat BELOW the accepted-ask branch of
`StoreStoreIssueRequest`, every arm of which ends in `continue`. They were therefore
unreachable on the only path the Store Issue Queue posts on. A `Nos.` item took `12.5` and
landed 12.5 trays in Production/WIP, 201 Created; a `Kgs.` unit was accepted and persisted
onto a `Nos.` item and read back in place of it.

`TwoUnitsChainTest` stayed green throughout, because every assertion in it posted without a
`material_request_line_id`. **A unit rule is only proven on the paths the tests walk**, and
the one they skipped was the one in daily use. Both are now refused, the guards run for
every line on both paths, and the placement is pinned by a mutation test. Full account in
`RELEASE-READINESS.md`.

The browser-side mirror is `permitsFractions()` in `frontend/src/features/material-flow/
words.ts` — the storekeeper's quantity input now refuses to accept half a tray. The server
is still the authority; that only stops the figure being typed.

## Field names — classified, and mostly to be left alone

Per the owner's ordering: establish the contract, add generic handling, and only then consider
names. **No historical field is renamed or migrated for cleanliness.**

| Class | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Legitimately weight-specific** | `material_lots.bag_weight_kg`, `material_bags.original_kg`/`remaining_kg`, `store_issue_bag_scans.quantity_kg`, `shift_scraps.quantity_kg`, `resin_pool_balances.*`, `batch_resin_allocations.quantity_kg` | **none** — these really are weights |
| **Generic quantity, misnamed** | `shift_material_consumptions.quantity_issued_kg` (**the headline**), `shift_stock_counts.quantity_kg`, `shift_production_entries.quantity_produced_kg`/`quantity_rejection_kg`, `material_lots.total_received_kg` | eventual rename, **after** the contract and generic handling land |
| **UI-only** | `ProductionQcPage.tsx:44`, `DashboardPage.tsx:62`, `PurchaseOrderTraceDrawer.tsx:227`, `CompleteBatchRequest.php:200`, `ProductReadinessService.php:74`, `cartonLabel.ts:43` | render `item.uom` — `formatQuantity()` is the in-repo pattern. **Pre-existing and outside PR #196**; deliberately not swept in on the eve of a deploy |
| **Historical compatibility** | the `short_kg` snapshot key | **none** — already half-fixed by adding `item_uom` beside it while keeping the key. That is the model for the rest |

### The headline, proven rather than inferred

`shift_material_consumptions.quantity_issued_kg` has **no unit gate at all** —
`CompleteBatchRequest` validates only `numeric, gt:0`. A carton line writes its *piece count*
into a kg-named column. Three separate comments in the codebase already admit it ("a batch
that issued 13,333 caps has a 13,333 'kg' consumption row").

**The number is not wrong — the name is.** The value is unit-less and Tally applies the item's
own unit on posting (the voucher builder emits no unit key at all). The defect bites only
where something *labels, sums or reasons about* it as kilograms.

## Known blockers for a non-weight material

| Stage | Status |
|---|---|
| PO · stock · store issue · Production/WIP panel | **clear** — unit-agnostic, and the panel is uom-driven |
| **GRN lot/bag capture** | **refused for non-kg items** ("Bag lots are only supported for items measured in kg"). The receipt completes; the lot/bag *trace* does not exist for counted materials |
| Material request | requires `is_production_input`; a newly created counted material is `false` until configured (Q56) |
| Day-bin / bin-bay screens | filter by kg unit, so a counted material is invisible there |
