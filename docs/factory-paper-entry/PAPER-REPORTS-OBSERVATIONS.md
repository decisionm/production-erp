# Paper-report observations — notes, not knowledge

Working notes from reading the photographed 04/05-Aug paper reports
(`docs/factory/sources/paper-reports/`, registered in
`docs/factory/sources/manifest.json`). These are OBSERVATIONS for follow-up
checks — nothing here is an owner decision; anything needing the factory's
answer lives in `docs/factory/PENDING-OWNER-QUESTIONS.md`.

## Rejection vocabulary on the paper forms (for the ScrapReason config check)

The three photographed shifts write rejection reasons in a small recurring
vocabulary. Worth checking the ERP's ScrapReason options cover these before
paper entry starts:

- `Stup Rej - P.F` (startup rejection)
- `Drop out`
- `M/c startup`
- `MDCH / startup`
- `Weak Bottle`
- `Cavity out`
- `String`
- `processing`

Several appear combined ("Startup / Drop out", "Startup / processing").
The forms record ONE reason (or pair) per machine per shift, not per-reject
counts.

## ASB-2 quality signal across the photographed shifts

ASB-2 (90 ml rib) had a visibly bad run of days, written across three
different sections of the paper forms:

- 04-Aug Shift B (back page): breakdown "HR cavity Zone-1 — flash issue.
  Cavity removed and silicon pasted", 2:00–7:00 (5 h), plus a 2 h manpower
  shortage.
- 04-Aug Shift C (front): 2,115 rejects against 5,280 produced (~40%).
- 05-Aug Shift A (back page): "Mould Breakdown" 11:30–1:15 (1:45); another
  1,072 rejects that shift.

The paper reader has to join a breakdown note, a rejection count and a
reason cell across pages to see this one story — the ERP's QC screens
would have surfaced it automatically (reject % by machine, downtime per
mold, both against the same batch). Good concrete example for the QC
rollout conversation.
