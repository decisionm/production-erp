The ERP surfaces only the modules this factory has ADOPTED — inventory, production, procurement, quality, tally-sync, sales, maintenance, compliance, plus users/roles administration — and hides CRM, Finance, HRMS and Payroll everywhere a module surfaces (sidebar and dashboard), whatever the user's permissions say, until the factory actually uses them. Nothing is deleted or disabled: every hidden module's screens and endpoints keep working, and adoption is a one-line source edit (ADOPTED_MODULES, frontend/src/lib/adoptedModules.ts) made the day a module goes into use — deliberately in source, not a settings toggle anyone can flip on a live floor. Grounds: working screens with no data read as a half-built product (the manager's 05-Aug complaint), and adoption is judged by counting rows ON THE LIVE INSTANCE, never dev — HRMS was nearly kept on the strength of 7 employee rows that were dev-database seed fixtures the owner called 'dummy data'. The dashboard additionally gates every figure server-side by the user's role.
