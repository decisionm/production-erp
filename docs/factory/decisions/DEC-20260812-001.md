HRMS and Payroll are adopted and visible in the ERP; CRM and Finance stay hidden. This keeps the adoption principle of DEC-20260809-001 — the ERP surfaces only modules the factory has adopted, adoption is a one-line source edit in ADOPTED_MODULES, and nothing is ever deleted or disabled — and changes only which modules are in that list. The grounds for un-hiding HRMS are that hiding it never protected anything: Employee is what shift_production_entries.operator_id, supervisor_signed_by and ShiftSummary.supervisor point at, so the Start Batch operator dropdown has been offering the seven demo people (EMP-001..EMP-007) to the floor all along. Those seven rows are therefore KEPT, NOT DELETED: the factory edits them into real people on the Employees screen, which fixes the operator dropdown at the same time and preserves every existing link from real production records. A demo NAME is a label the factory overwrites; a demo POSTING is a false record, so demo transactions against those people (attendance, leave requests, payroll runs, payslips) are listed for the owner and cleared only on his word, separately. Leave types, salary components, a simple monthly salary structure and current-year leave balances are seeded on the owner's instruction using ordinary Indian factory standards (Casual Leave 12 days, Sick Leave 12, Earned/Privilege Leave 15; Basic, HRA and allowances with PF and ESI as deductions) as a STARTING POINT ONLY — every seeded row must be visibly marked as a default the factory has not yet confirmed, in the same spirit as the downtime reasons' To Confirm marker, because a number nobody has agreed to must never look like a number somebody agreed to. The factory's real policy replaces these; the open questions (which leave types are actually given, days per year for each, carry-forward, monthly versus daily-wage versus piece-rate, OT rate, PF/ESI applicability, payroll period and payday, and whether payroll should ever post to Tally) are recorded as pending, not answered by the defaults. CRM stays hidden until the factory records its first real enquiry, since opening three empty screens is the complaint the adoption rule exists to prevent.
