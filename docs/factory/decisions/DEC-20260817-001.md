The factory's logical inventory locations are Raw Material Store -> Production/WIP -> Finished Goods Store. There is no Day Bin. Production/WIP is the inventory location holding material that has been physically issued to production but is not yet consumed, which is what makes 'issued to production' a real stock state distinct from both store stock and consumption. Additional warehouses stay configurable, but only where there is a genuine additional physical or business location: synonymous duplicates must not be created. The existing duplicate rows (FG alongside FG-STORE, RM alongside RM-STORE) are therefore residue to be resolved, not two real places each; no merge, deletion or deactivation of them happens until dependency and stock history have been proven for each row, because the transactional history and the Tally identity currently sit on different rows of each pair.
