Material that has bags standing in incoming-QC hold (waiting_qc) may not leave a store's stock balance through any outflow door: not a Store Issue, not a generic stock issue, not a stock transfer, not a production completion's material consumption, and not a Tally stock reconciliation. The single exception is QA's rejection at incoming inspection, which may remove the quantity that inspection has actually rejected, and nothing more - no other actor and no other route may remove held material. QA's ACCEPTANCE is not a removal: accepted bags simply leave the hold and become issuable like any other stock. This is a hold on ISSUANCE only - it does not defer, condition or otherwise change GRN stock posting. The hold reaches only material that actually HAS bags in waiting_qc; this decision creates no hold for material that has none. Three things are expressly NOT decided here and remain open: what carries the barcode for counted packaging, what Tally should receive when material is rejected at QA (an accepted-only Receipt Note, or a full Receipt Note plus a separate rejection voucher), and whether every arrival line must wait for QA before the store may issue it or only named materials.
