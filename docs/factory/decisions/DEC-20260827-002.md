The ERP item taxonomy is the SEVEN the owner named: Raw Material, Packing Material, Finished Good, Work in Progress, Consumable, Spare or Tooling, and Other — with unclassified (NULL) remaining a real and distinct state meaning 'not recorded yet', never 'none of the above'. The last three (Work in Progress, Consumable, Spare or Tooling) were added to the enum by the product-identity build on the owner's written instruction and are recorded here because a category an editor can select and store is a real addition to the factory's taxonomy, not a label: an agent may not widen it on its own. NOTHING IS AUTO-ASSIGNED TO THE NEW THREE. DEC-20260827-001 maps Tally stock groups to raw material, packing material, finished good and other only, so no group proposes Work in Progress, Consumable or Spare/Tooling, and the twelve items in no Tally group (a pen drive, a servo amplifier, mould-release spray, 3D prototypes, a Tally licence) stay NULL rather than being filed under the new names on an agent's reading. A person selects one deliberately or it is never set. This does not change document eligibility: which categories each document may use is Q59 and stays open.
