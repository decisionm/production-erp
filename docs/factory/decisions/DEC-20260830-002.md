The factory operates ONE physical Godown/Store, and its books carry one godown. Raw material, packing material, work in progress and finished goods all belong to that single operational Store; they are distinguished by inventory CATEGORY, STOCK STATE or INTERNAL LOCATION, never by separate physical Tally godowns. This supersedes DEC-20260817-001's reading of 'Raw Material Store -> Production/WIP -> Finished Goods Store' as three separate places. It expressly does NOT supersede the invariant that decision protects, which stands unchanged: 'issued to production but not yet consumed' remains a real stock state, distinct from both store stock and from consumption, and it is carried by the Production/WIP INTERNAL LOCATION - the warehouse row coded WIP - which must remain resolvable by the application whether or not any picker offers it. Folding that row's balances into the Store would destroy the only state separating the floor from the store and is NOT authorised by this decision. Also unchanged and still in force: the duplicate warehouse rows named by DEC-20260817-001 are not to be merged, deleted or deactivated until dependency and stock history have been proven for each row, and what becomes of stock standing in a retired row is a separate owner decision.
