A stock movement reference recorded against a retired warehouse - a goods receipt, a QC release to store, a shift production entry, a delivery - is accepted as proof that the material it describes is physically standing in that location today. Recovering such a row into the single operational Store therefore does NOT require a physical count first. This answers the first half of PENDING Q74. Two things are expressly NOT settled by it and each still blocks the move on its own: which reference WORDINGS count as such a document (the preview's DOCUMENTED classification is the agent's reading and remains for the owner to confirm against the actual list before any row moves), and the disposition of the opening-balance and wiring-check rows, which is Q75 and still open. The costing consequence of a recovery is Q76 and also still open. Nothing in this decision authorises a move; it removes only the physical-count precondition.
