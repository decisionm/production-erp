The stock screen shows four figures per row: On hand, QA hold, Reserved, and Free to issue. 'Free to issue' is on hand less the incoming-QC hold and less outstanding customer reservations, clamped at zero. This is DELIBERATELY STRICTER than the engine: StockMovementService's outflow check subtracts the incoming-QC hold and does NOT subtract reservations, so the system will still permit issuing stock that is promised to a customer. The screen under-reports on purpose - a storekeeper who means to break a reservation may still do it, but must not do it by accident. The three component figures are shown beside the headline so nothing is hidden and the difference from the engine is visible rather than silent. This resolves PENDING Q79. It changes no stock arithmetic and no write path; it is a reading of existing figures.
