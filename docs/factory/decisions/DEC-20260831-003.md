A purchase order holds quantity against its purchase requisition only once it has been SENT to the vendor. A DRAFT order reserves nothing: the requisition's Balance to Order is unchanged while a buyer is still typing, and a draft may be typed for any quantity. The consequence, accepted when this was decided: two drafts may each be raised for the whole requisition and the second is refused at Send, after the vendor and the rates have been typed, rather than at typing. The ERP therefore enforces the combined-quantity rule in PurchaseOrderService::send(), not at create or amend, and a guard on those paths would be unreachable rather than merely redundant.
