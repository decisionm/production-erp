Dispatch of finished goods is gated on INTERNAL QUALITY APPROVAL, and there is NO separate customer-approval step. The sequence is: stock is fully held against the sales order line, then Quality approves, then Sales dispatches, then Sales issues the invoice. Quality's approval must be RECORDED against the line — who approved, when, and for what quantity — not merely asserted, and a line may not be dispatched beyond the quantity Quality approved. This resolves Q27, which had left dispatch of not-yet-approved batches deliberately open since 07-Aug-2026 and is answered here as option (b), the QC pass required; it narrows DEC-20260807-013, under which a quality-REJECTED carton was refused at dispatch while a batch merely not yet through QC passed freely. Expressly settled: the customer-approval gate that earlier fulfilment work carried as an unbuilt 'not recorded' column does not exist and is to be removed rather than built, so no ERP screen, column or condition may imply a customer sign-off the factory does not perform. Expressly NOT decided here: whether the Store, rather than Sales, performs the final dispatch act (Q78 remains open) and whether Quality may withdraw an approval after goods have moved.
