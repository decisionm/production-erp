Material that came out of the store on a Store Issue must be returned against that exact Store Issue. This applies to open, partially returned AND completed Store Issues, for as long as returnable quantity remains on the issue. Completing a Store Issue moves no stock, so a completed issue with outstanding quantity is still holding material on the floor and still owns its return.
