At the end of production the storekeeper may return part or all of the unused material from Production/WIP to the Store; a partial return is as valid as a full one. If the material originally came through a Store Issue, the return MUST identify that exact Store Issue, so the handover's own quantity closes and the ledger records which handover the material came back on. Material standing in Production/WIP with NO Store Issue behind it may be returned without one — the rule reaches as far as a Store Issue exists to be named, and never requires inventing one. Material that is not returned REMAINS AVAILABLE in Production/WIP and is the next day's opening material on the floor; it is not consumed, not written off, and not moved by the passing of a day. When the next material request is prepared, the ERP MUST take account of the usable quantity already standing in Production/WIP for the SAME item and the SAME unit of measure, and the request screen must show three figures: the total material required, the quantity already available in Production/WIP, and the balance to request from the Store. The balance to request is the total required minus the usable quantity already in Production/WIP, floored at zero — a floor already covered on the production side means nothing is asked of the Store. Usable is narrower than present: a negative Production/WIP balance is a discrepancy and is netted as zero, and a quantity standing in a unit the item master no longer agrees with is reported but never subtracted (FC-03).
