A material request is raised for the NET quantity: net = max(0, total required - usable Production/WIP balance for the same item and UOM). Usable means the Production/WIP balance floored at zero, because a negative balance is an over-draw and not a supply. The line stores the net as its quantity, since every fulfilment figure is computed against it, and keeps the total required beside it so the netting can be read back. A line netting to zero is kept, not dropped. KNOWN LIMIT OF THE RULE AS STATED: nothing reserves the floor balance, so two requests raised before either is issued both net against the same kilograms and the second under-asks.
