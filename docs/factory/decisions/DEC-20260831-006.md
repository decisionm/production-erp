A purchase order's hold on its requisition follows the material, not the paperwork. A DRAFT purchase order reserves ZERO quantity — nothing is held until the order goes to the vendor. An OPEN order (Sent or Partially Received) reserves its FULL ordered quantity, because the vendor is expected to deliver all of it. When an order finally reaches Closed or Cancelled, only the quantity RECEIVED AND ACCEPTED BY QUALITY remains consumed against the requisition; every undelivered quantity — and every quantity rejected by Quality and not replaced — returns to the original requisition's Balance to Order and may be ordered again. This SUPERSEDES DEC-20260831-002, which ruled that a sent-and-cancelled order kept its full allowance: the principle is that a requisition is consumed by material actually delivered and accepted, never by an order that failed to deliver it, so a vendor's shortfall or a cancellation costs the factory no requisition. The practical consequence is that Balance to Order can rise when an order closes short, which is correct and intended.
