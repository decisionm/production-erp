Material rejected at incoming Quality has exactly two dispositions, and the buyer chooses. VENDOR REPLACEMENT keeps the rejected quantity PENDING ON THE SAME PURCHASE ORDER: the order reopens (it is not finished, because the vendor still owes that material), the rejected quantity is not treated as received, and the replacement arrives as a NEW goods receipt against that same order and passes a new Quality inspection like any other arrival. DEBIT NOTE ends the vendor's obligation for that quantity: it is removed from the billable quantity so the supplier bill cannot charge for it, and it returns to the original requisition's Balance to Order so the buyer may order it again — from this vendor or another. A rejection is therefore never silently absorbed: it either comes back as material or comes off the bill, and the requisition's balance tells the truth either way.
