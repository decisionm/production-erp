The ERP posts BOTH Delivery Notes and Sales Invoices to Tally. This reverses the direction DEC-20260809-003 recorded — that all real sales are invoiced directly in Tally and the ERP Sales module is demo-scale — because the ERP's Sales module is going live to production and becomes the origin of the sale. It resolves Q70 (the factory's Tally held zero Delivery Note vouchers, and none of its 177 real Sales vouchers referenced one; the ERP now introduces them as a new practice rather than mirroring an old one) and Q71 (which book originates the sale: the ERP). The posting stays FAIL-CLOSED and that is part of the decision, not a caveat: when the customer's Tally ledger, an item's HSN or its rate, the sales or tax ledger mapping, the godown, or the allowed Tally company is missing or unconfigured, the ERP records a NAMED REFUSAL against the document and stages nothing. A refusal must never block the factory's own act — an invoice still issues and a delivery still dispatches when Tally staging refuses, because issuing and dispatching are the factory's business and posting to Tally is bookkeeping that follows. Expressly NOT decided here: which numbering series a Tally-bound ERP invoice carries — Tally owns a contiguous NNN/26-27 'Auto Renumber' series that the factory's receipts knock off against, and the ERP mints INV-{id}; and whether the ERP may also post a Sales ORDER voucher (Q72) is untouched.
