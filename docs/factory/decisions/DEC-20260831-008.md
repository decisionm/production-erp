A supplier bill may charge only for what Quality ACCEPTED. The billable quantity of a goods-receipt line is its QC-accepted quantity, not the quantity that physically arrived, so material rejected at incoming inspection is never billed; and EACH GOODS RECEIPT LINE IS BILLED ONCE — a second bill line against the same receipt line is refused. The ERP enforces both, rather than leaving them to whoever is reading the invoice.
