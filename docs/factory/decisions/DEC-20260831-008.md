The ERP does NOT send Sales Orders, Delivery Notes or Sales Invoices to Tally. This REVERSES DEC-20260831-007, which had turned ERP-originated Delivery Note and Sales Invoice posting ON earlier the same day; the direction of the sales integration is now inbound only. TALLY ORIGINATES THE SALE on the accounting side: Tally creates the Sales Invoice and produces the e-invoice (IRN) and e-way-bill details, which the ERP never fabricates and never posts. The ERP's job is to IMPORT the Tally Sales Invoice XML, MATCH each imported voucher to the ERP Sales Order it belongs to, and UPDATE the ERP Sales Order's status from that match. The matching key is the CUSTOMER plus the CUSTOMER'S OWN PURCHASE-ORDER REFERENCE (sales_orders.customer_po_reference), which the ERP already records: in the factory's own 31-Aug export Tally's Sales voucher carries that string in BASICPURCHASEORDERNO / REFERENCE / ORDERNO and its Sales Order voucher carries the identical string in ORDERNO / REFERENCE, joining 32 of 55 Sales vouchers to a Sales Order with ZERO ambiguous keys. Voucher NUMBER is expressly NOT the key - Tally owns a contiguous NNN/26-27 series while the ERP mints INV-{id}, so the two numbering series never meet. This also resolves Q61 and Q72 (may the ERP emit a Tally Sales ORDER voucher) as NO. The ERP remains the origin of the FACTORY act - the sales order, the stock hold, the production request, the quality approval and the dispatch that reduces ERP stock are all the ERP's and are unaffected; only the accounting document direction is reversed. Expressly NOT decided here: whether the Store rather than Sales performs the final dispatch act (Q78 stays open), and what the ERP should do with an imported Tally invoice that matches NO ERP sales order (it is recorded as unmatched for a person to resolve, and is not permitted to create an order).
