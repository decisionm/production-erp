Emergency material may be received WITHOUT a purchase order, after approval by an Admin or an Accountant. The approval is recorded on the receipt (who approved it and when), and from that point the material follows the ordinary path without exception: incoming Quality inspection, and a supplier bill limited to the QC-accepted quantity. An emergency receipt answers no requisition and therefore consumes no requisition balance. This resolves the standing question of whether material may be purchased without a purchase order: it may, but only on a named approval, and never as a way around Quality or billing.
