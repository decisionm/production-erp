A goods receipt must record what physically arrived — the lot and its bags — for every purchased material. IMPLEMENTED FOR WEIGHED MATERIALS ONLY, and the gap is not a choice: GoodsReceiptService refuses a lots block outright for any item not measured in kg, and the reconciliation beneath it is arithmetic in kilograms (a nominal or per-bag weight is mandatory, and bag_weight x bag_count must equal the received line quantity). Applied literally to a counted material the rule would REQUIRE a block the service REFUSES, so cartons, trays and film could not be received at all, and the only way to satisfy both rules would be to invent a kilogram weight for a carton and put a fiction into the stock ledger and into Tally. Weighed arrivals (resin, masterbatch) are therefore mandatory now; counted packaging is unchanged pending a unit-aware lot and an answer to what carries the barcode when one bag is a bundle of 500 cartons.
