Purchase Orders, Supplier Bills and Debit Notes are sent to Tally. Goods Receipts remain ERP-only and are never posted — this keeps DEC-20260830-001 (the factory does not use Tally Receipt Notes) intact and unchanged. The ERP's goods receipt stays the factory's arrival record; what reaches Tally is the order, the purchase invoice and the debit note, and all quantities and accounting records between the two books must reconcile. The Tally PURCHASE LEDGER is selected PER LINE from the vendor's state (local versus interstate) and the line's GST rate — it is never one global ledger, which DEC-20260812-003 already measured as wrong for this factory. Admin or Accounts configures the exact four ledger mappings in the ERP; the ERP selects among them by that rule and NEVER invents or defaults a ledger. A line whose ledger cannot be resolved refuses to post, with the reason named, rather than guessing. This resolves the standing purchase-ledger question and the standing question of whether the accountant wants ERP supplier bills posted to Tally.
