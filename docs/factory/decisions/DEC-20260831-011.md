The supplier bill's lifecycle separates CAPTURING the vendor's paper from COMMITTING to it. Accounts may save the vendor invoice as a DRAFT the moment it arrives, exactly as printed and for the full invoiced quantity — the ERP does not stand between Accounts and the paper on the desk, and an invoice that arrives with the lorry is the ordinary case. RECORDING that bill, posting it, or sending it to Tally is allowed only up to the CUMULATIVE QUANTITY ACCEPTED BY INCOMING QUALITY for each matched goods-receipt line. UNINSPECTED quantity contributes ZERO to the recordable amount, and REJECTED quantity contributes ZERO: material Quality has not passed is not material the factory owes for, and waiting for the inspection is the correct behaviour rather than an obstacle. A bill whose lines exceed the accepted quantity stays a draft until Quality catches up or the line is corrected. Each goods-receipt line is billed ONCE, across every bill. This SUPERSEDES DEC-20260831-008, which capped the bill at acceptance but treated an UNINSPECTED receipt as billable in full — that reading let a bill be recorded, and would have let it reach Tally, for material Quality had never passed.
