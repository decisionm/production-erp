BOTH a partial and a full end-of-day return are allowed, and a FULL RETURN IS NOT MANDATORY. The Store may return part or all of the unused material at the end of the day, and material that is NOT returned remains in Production/WIP and carries forward as the next day's material on the floor. This CONFIRMS DEC-20260831-005 rather than changing it, and it is recorded rather than left implicit because a later owner instruction read as a mandatory end-of-day return: the two could not both be true, and an agent may not resolve that ambiguity by choosing. Concretely the ERP must NOT require Production/WIP to be empty at the end of a shift, must NOT refuse or flag a floor for being non-empty, and must keep netting the next material request against the usable quantity standing there (the three figures of DEC-20260831-005: total required, already available in Production/WIP, and the balance to request). This resolves PENDING Q88. NO CODE CHANGE FOLLOWS FROM IT - the shipped behaviour is already exactly this, which is why the answer is recorded as a confirmation and nothing was built or withdrawn to satisfy it.
