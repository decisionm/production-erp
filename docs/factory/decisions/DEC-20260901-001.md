THE STORE performs the final dispatch action, and SALES does not. This resolves Q78, which DEC-20260831-006 left expressly open ('whether the Store, rather than Sales, performs the final dispatch act'). The sequence DEC-20260831-006 fixed is otherwise unchanged and every gate in it stands: stock is held in full against the sales order line, then internal Quality approves and that approval is RECORDED against the line (who, when, for what quantity), then the goods are dispatched, then Sales issues the invoice. What moves is only WHO performs the dispatch: the act now belongs to the Store, the team that physically has the goods and prepares them, and the desk that sold them may no longer perform it. Everything the dispatch already did it still does - barcode/carton validation, the refusal of a quality-REJECTED carton (DEC-20260807-013), the cap at the quantity Quality approved, the single stock issue that reduces ERP stock, and the audit of who dispatched. Sales KEEPS its read of deliveries: it must still be able to see and trace what left against its orders, and reading is not dispatching. The permission consequence is deliberate and narrow: dispatch is gated on the STORE's own inventory permission, and the Store is NOT granted sales.manage or any wider Sales access to obtain it - the same two-sided shape Quality's dispatch sign-off already uses, where the record lives on a Sales model but the act is gated to the team that performs it. Expressly NOT decided here: whether Quality may withdraw an approval after goods have moved (still open from DEC-20260831-006), and whether the Store may also CANCEL or reverse a dispatch it made.
