MATERIAL SUBSTITUTION IS RECORDED AS TWO LINES, NEVER AS ONE EDITED LINE. When the floor cannot get all of a required material and an alternate is used in its place, the ORIGINAL line keeps the quantity of the original material that was ACTUALLY used, and the alternate material is added as a SEPARATE CONTROLLED LINE against the same requirement. The owner's worked example is the rule: required 6, original actually used 5, alternate used 1, total actual 6. The original line's actual quantity stays 5 and must never be written up to 6 - a single netted line of 6 would say the requirement was met by the original material, which is not what happened, and the whole reason to record a substitution is that it happened. WHAT THIS CHANGES, STATED ACCURATELY: until now the ERP REFUSED a substitution outright. StoreStoreIssueRequest rejected any issue line whose item differed from the item its material-request line asked for, with the message 'This line hands over a different material from the one the request asked for.' There was therefore NO way to record a substitution at all, and a floor that used an alternate either could not book the handover or had to book it against no requirement, losing the link to what was required. This decision opens that door, and opens it only under control. CONTROLLED means the substitute line must NAME WHAT IT STANDS IN FOR AND WHY: the item it substitutes for, and a reason, both recorded against the line. A line whose item differs from its requirement's item and which carries NO reason stays REFUSED exactly as it is today - the door opens for a recorded substitution and for nothing else, so a mis-picked line is still caught. The requirement's fulfilment counts BOTH lines: 5 of the original plus 1 of the alternate closes a requirement for 6, because the floor got what it needed. THREE THINGS ARE EXPRESSLY NOT DECIDED HERE. (1) WHICH items may substitute for which - there is no substitution master and none is created; any item that is otherwise issuable may be recorded as a substitute and the recorded reason is the only control, so nothing here validates a substitution against an engineering rule. (2) Whether a substitution needs an APPROVER above the person recording it - a reason and an actor are recorded, an approval workflow is not built, and if the factory wants a second signature that is a separate decision. (3) Whether RESIN substitution is permitted at all - resin is governed by FC-01 and the common-input rules, and nothing here is to be read as authorising a resin substitute.
