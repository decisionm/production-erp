DAMAGED FINISHED GOODS BECOME SCRAP. A finished good found damaged is not written off to nothing and is not returned to stock as a saleable finished good: it becomes Scrap, which this factory already treats as real stock booked inward per colour (FC-02, and 33 inward Scrap lines across the 38 real Stock Journals). This is a clarification the owner gave while narrowing Q89, and it settles the FINISHED GOOD half of 'what happens to damaged material' only. THREE THINGS ARE EXPRESSLY NOT DECIDED HERE. (1) The disposition of damaged RAW MATERIAL, PACKING MATERIAL or CONSUMABLES returned from Production stays OPEN as the narrowed Q89 - whether the Store may re-issue such material as it stands, whether it must wait for Quality, or whether it leaves stock as a write-off - and no agent may read this finished-goods answer across onto them. (2) Nothing here authorises building a finished-good-to-scrap conversion flow, a screen or a Tally posting; no such path exists today and creating one is a separate, deliberate piece of work. (3) Which Scrap item a given damaged finished good becomes is not decided here: ScrapItemResolver resolves scrap per colour for production output, and whether a damaged finished good follows the same mapping is unanswered - a scrap item name is a live Tally master and is never invented by an agent.
