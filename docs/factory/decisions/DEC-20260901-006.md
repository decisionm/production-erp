THE DAMAGED FINISHED GOOD REACHES SCRAP THE SAME WAY DAMAGED RETURNED MATERIAL DOES: through Quality, never straight out of stock. DEC-20260901-002 settled that a damaged finished good BECOMES SCRAP but expressly did not authorise building the path, leaving it as a separate deliberate piece of work; the owner has now asked for that path, so this decision authorises it and nothing wider. THE FLOW. The Store reports a finished good as damaged; the quantity moves OUT OF THE FINISHED-GOODS STORE and INTO THE QUALITY-HOLD LOCATION, so it is out of issuable, sellable and dispatchable stock from the moment it is reported, by construction rather than by a filter. Quality then does exactly what it already does for returned material: CONFIRM DAMAGE, which scraps the quantity, or RELEASE it back to a store when it looks and the goods are fine. Reporting is not scrapping and the Store cannot scrap on its own - only Quality's confirmation takes the quantity out of stock, which is the whole meaning of 'to Quality first'. IT REUSES THE SIBLING PATH RATHER THAN COPYING IT. The quality-hold location (DEC-20260901-003), the standing-in-hold read, confirm-damage and release are the SAME code that already serves damaged returned material; those operate on an item and a location and never cared which kind of item it was. Only the DOOR IN is new, because until now the only way into quality hold was material coming back off the floor. Two behaviours a copy would have drifted on are therefore free: a hold is bounded by what is actually standing, and dispositions move in item order so two of them cannot deadlock. WHICH SCRAP ITEM IS STILL NOT DECIDED, and this decision does not need it. Scrap is an ISSUE OUT OF STOCK carrying purpose 'scrap', not a change of item identity and not an inward booking against a Scrap master - the same route incoming QC uses for material it rejects. DEC-20260901-002's third boundary therefore stands untouched: no scrap item is named, and no Tally master is invented. NOTHING IS POSTED TO TALLY. What Tally should receive when a finished good is scrapped is undecided, exactly as it is for scrapped returned material (DEC-20260901-003) and for QA rejection at arrival (DEC-20260825-001). The stock fact is recorded and the voucher waits for the answer. THE DOOR IS FINISHED GOODS ONLY. A line naming an item that is not a finished good is refused and pointed at the returned-material door, so the two paths cannot be used interchangeably and DEC-20260901-002's finished-goods answer is not quietly read across onto raw or packing material, whose disposition DEC-20260901-003 settles separately. EXPRESSLY NOT DECIDED: whether a damaged finished good already promised to a customer should release its reservation when reported, and whether a dispatched carton found damaged AFTER it left may come back through this door - both are returns from a customer, which this factory has no path for at all.
