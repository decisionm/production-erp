The controlled dropdown behind an off-plan ACTUAL-CONSUMPTION line at production completion offers ANY ACTIVE STOCK ITEM - not a category-restricted list, and not a pre-declared substitute-pair master. The owner was asked to choose between same-category-only, any active item, an owner-approved substitute list, and no substitution at all, and chose any active item. THIS SETS BREADTH ONLY AND REPEALS NOTHING. DEC-20260901-004 remains the rule for HOW a substitution is recorded anywhere in the flow, and this line obeys it in full: the substitute NAMES WHAT IT STOOD IN FOR AND WHY, both against the line (shift_material_consumptions.substitutes_item_id and .substitution_reason, the same two columns and the same 'is a substitution when the item is set' predicate as store_issue_lines), the ORIGINAL line keeps its own short actual figure and is never written up, and the two lines are never merged - merging would put a quantity of one item against another item's ledger in Tally. THE TWO SURFACES ARE DISTINCT AND NEITHER REPLACES THE OTHER: a store issue substitution is what the STOREKEEPER HANDED OVER, one document earlier; a consumption substitution is what the MACHINE ACTUALLY ATE, and a run may consume material off the floor that no store issue covered that day. Recording one requires the material-substitution.manage permission. That is a WHO-MAY-RECORD rule and is expressly NOT the approver-above-the-recorder that DEC-20260901-004 leaves undecided and unbuilt; no approval workflow exists. The dropdown is deliberately stricter than the engine: it offers only items with usable Production/WIP stock by DEC-20260831-005's definition (a negative balance nets as zero, a unit the item master disagrees with is reported but never counted, FC-03) and less the incoming-QC hold, because DEC-20260825-001 forbids material with bags in waiting_qc leaving through a production completion. Expressly NOT decided: which items SHOULD substitute for which - no substitution master exists and none is created, no interchangeability is asserted between any two items, and FC-03 (tape stays display-only), FC-07 (clear takes no masterbatch) and DEC-20260825-001 all stand unchanged.
