Recording a production consumption line for a material the run was NOT planned on is the office's authority, not the floor's: the permission consumption-substitute.manage is held by PLANT MANAGER and ACCOUNTS (and by the owner, who logs in as Administrator and holds every catalogue permission). A Supervisor completes batches on production.manage as before and is NOT given it; where a supervisor needs to book a substitute material, a plant manager or Accounts records that line. These are the same two roles the production approval chain already checks by name and the same two DEC-20260810-001 named for the internal carton-trace tier, so the grant lands on identities the factory already treats as the office. The grant is ADDITIVE in PermissionSeeder (givePermissionTo, never syncPermissions), so it never rewrites what an administrator has configured through the Roles screen, and it is the .manage half only - the .view twin gates no route. Expressly NOT decided here: whether a production completion may refuse a material on its CATEGORY (spares, tooling, unclassified masters) remains PENDING Q90 and open, and this decision switches on no such refusal. What an added line DOES is unchanged and additive: the material it stood in for keeps its own line at its own quantity, and nothing reduces or clears it.
