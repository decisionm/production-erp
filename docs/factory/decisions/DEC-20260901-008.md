AT PRODUCTION COMPLETION, A SUBSTITUTED MATERIAL IS AN ADDED LINE AND NOTHING IS EVER REDUCED. When a run books actual consumption of a material it was NOT planned on - the 100 ml cartons ran out, so the run packed in 90 ml boxes - the material it stood in for KEEPS ITS OWN LINE at whatever quantity was actually issued, which is often zero, and the substitute is recorded as a SEPARATE line beside it carrying its own reason and the user who authorised it. No code path reduces, clears, nets or reassigns the original line, and both lines reach the Tally Stock Journal exactly as recorded. A single netted line would say the run consumed what it was planned to consume, which is the one thing that did not happen, and a substitution nobody can see afterwards is the failure this whole path exists to prevent. THIS IS THE COMPLETION DOOR, and it is the sibling of DEC-20260901-004 rather than a repeat of it: that decision governs the STORE ISSUE door, where a substitute line is issued against a material-request line and therefore inherits WHAT IT STANDS IN FOR from the requirement it answers. A completion line answers no requirement document, so there is nothing for it to inherit, and the owner was asked about this door specifically and chose a reason ALONE over a reason plus an explicit stored link to the displaced material. Consequence, stated plainly rather than left to be discovered: a completion's added line records WHY but not WHICH material it replaced, so the two cannot be joined automatically on a report - they are two lines on one batch, read together by a person. Whether the completion line should also name the material it stands in for is NOT decided here and may be asked later; adding it would be a new field, not a change to this rule. Also unchanged: WHICH materials may be booked at all is the controlled list, and whether that list may refuse on item CATEGORY remains PENDING Q90.
