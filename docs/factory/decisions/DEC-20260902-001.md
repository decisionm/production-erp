The Store has ONE role and it is called 'Store' (it was first defined as 'Storekeeper' on 30-Aug-2026; the definition renames a live 'Storekeeper' role in place so its holders keep it). The Store role holds inventory.view and inventory.manage, procurement.view and procurement.manage, production.view and quality.view — and nothing else. PROCUREMENT IS GRANTED IN FULL: the procurement module has one write permission that gates vendors, purchase requisitions and their approval, purchase orders (raise, send, amend, close, cancel) and goods receipts alike, so the Store may do all of those; the owner was told there is no narrower grant that would allow a goods receipt alone and chose full procurement. FC-06 stands: purchase-order and goods-receipt lines withhold every rate from a reader without a finance permission, and supplier bills and vendor-item rates sit behind the finance module, so a Store login sees quantities and never a price. NO HRMS: the owner said in as many words 'no hrms for Store', and the Store role holds no hrms.view or hrms.manage; there is no employee self-service scope in HRMS today that would let a login see only its own record, and none is implied here. The Store role also never holds finance.*, users.manage, roles.manage or configuration-delete.* (DEC-20260817-002 §3). The dashboard needs no grant of its own: every login reaches it and each block shows by the module permissions the reader holds. Vasanth is the first holder of the Store role, on the owner's instruction, and holds it through his existing login — the role definition assigns nobody unless one existing, active login is named exactly, and never creates a login. The store-acceptance half of Q78 remains OPEN and is not decided here.
