THE STORE SCANS THE BAG ONCE, AT THE STORE ISSUE, AND THE FLOOR DOES NOT SCAN AGAIN. Material reaches the floor through a Store Issue: the Store scans each bag's inward barcode as it issues the bag, the Store Issue moves that material from the Store into the Production/WIP internal location (DEC-20260830-002, DEC-20260831-005), and the bag then goes to the centralised day-bin load with NO second scan by the supervisor. This supersedes the load-time scan clause of DEC-20260807-006 ('the supervisor scans the inward-generated bag barcode at load'). Everything else in DEC-20260807-006 stands unchanged and is restated here so it is not lost: the per-machine Bin Bay page is dead; the floor's only resin flow is ONE centralised day bin, crane-fed and piped to all machines; consumption is derived per batch at completion; bag-to-batch identity is physically impossible and is never claimed (FC-01); historical machine-stamped day_bin_movements rows stay untouched. DEC-20260807-007 (the day-bin figure is a drifting estimate, never counted) and DEC-20260810-001 (resin provenance is the calculated day-bin attribution) are unchanged in rule. EXPRESSLY NOT DECIDED HERE, and placed in PENDING: (1) whether the ERP keeps a separate day-bin balance beside the Production/WIP balance, fed by the Store's scanned bags, or treats Production/WIP as the day bin; (2) whether the floor records anything at all when it tips an issued bag into the bin. Whether the current Store Issue screen already scans bags is a code verification, not part of this rule.
