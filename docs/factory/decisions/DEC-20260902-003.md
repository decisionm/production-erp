MATERIAL THAT GOES INTO THE COMMON DAY BIN IS NEVER RETURNED TO THE STORE; EVERY OTHER MATERIAL MAY BE SENT BACK AT THE END OF THE DAY. The rule is by FLOW, not by item name: once material has been tipped into the centralised day bin - PET resin is the case the owner named first, and anything else that goes into the bin is treated the same - it is not returned, not partially, not fully, not at the end of the day; it stays on the floor and is consumed from there. Material that is still in its bag, box or roll - packing material, and any other input that does not go into the bin - may be sent back at the end of the day, partially or fully, exactly as DEC-20260831-005 and DEC-20260901-001 provide. This NARROWS those two decisions to non-bin materials and changes nothing else in them: a return still names the Store Issue that put the material there (DEC-20260831-008), a full return is still not mandatory, material not returned still remains in Production/WIP as the next day's opening material, and the next request is still netted against the usable Production/WIP quantity for the same item and unit - bin materials included, since they stay on the floor. A damaged return of a returnable material still goes to quality inspection (DEC-20260901-003). Consequence for the ERP: the return screen must refuse a bin-material line with a plain message, never hide it and never accept it. EXPRESSLY NOT DECIDED HERE: how the ERP knows which items go into the day bin once the floor no longer scans at load (DEC-20260902-002) - today that fact is carried only by the act of scanning a bag at the bin, and no item flag records it; this is added to PENDING Q94 as clause (c). Whether masterbatch goes into the bin is a fact about the floor that no primary source states, and it is not decided here.
