THE FINISHED-GOODS QUALITY CHECKLIST IS EVIDENCE BESIDE THE COUNT, NEVER A SECOND STOCK PATH. The Quality screen used after a batch is completed shows the batch and records the sample count, the measured weight, the standard weight (the run's frozen unit weight, DEC-20260805-005) and the visual observations. That record supports the decision and changes nothing in stock. The existing reviewed, OK and rejected count remains the ONLY action that changes finished-goods stock and books rejected pieces as scrap (FC-02, DEC-20260805-001), under the four-eyes rule already in force (FC-05). Expressly NOT decided here, each to be asked on its own: the weight tolerance, the sample-size rule, and whether visual observations are a maintained list or free text.
