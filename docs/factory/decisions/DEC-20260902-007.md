THE FINISHED-GOODS WEIGHT CHECK USES A PER-PRODUCT TOLERANCE, HAS NO DEFAULT, AND NEVER MOVES STOCK. The frozen standard weight is grams per piece (the run's frozen unit weight, DEC-20260805-005). On the Quality screen (DEC-20260902-006) Quality records the sample count and the TOTAL measured sample weight; the ERP calculates the average measured weight per piece as total sample weight divided by sample count, and compares that average with the frozen standard weight using the tolerance configured for THAT PRODUCT. The tolerance is stored on the product's standard and entered by an authorised person; there is NO default tolerance and no factory-wide band. Until the product's tolerance is configured, the screen shows the standard weight and the measured average and gives NO pass/fail verdict. An out-of-tolerance result is a WARNING and Quality evidence only: it must not automatically move stock, book scrap, reject the batch or block the approval chain. The Quality checker still enters the final OK and rejected count, which remains the only stock and scrap action. Expressly NOT decided here: the tolerance figure for any product - master data a person enters, never a value an agent invents.
