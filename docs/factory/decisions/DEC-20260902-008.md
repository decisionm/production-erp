VISUAL OBSERVATIONS ON THE FINISHED-GOODS QUALITY SCREEN ARE A MAINTAINED LIST PLUS ONE NOTE, NEVER FREE TEXT ALONE. On the Quality screen after a completed batch (DEC-20260902-006) the checker records what was seen on the sample by ticking observation types from a list, optionally with a count per type, and may add one free note. The list is MASTER DATA: its entries are entered and maintained by an authorised person under the existing configuration permission, and no entry is invented by an agent or seeded from a guess; until a person has entered the list, the screen offers the note alone. A ticked observation is Quality evidence only: it does not move stock, book scrap, reject the batch or block the approval chain; the OK and rejected count remains the only stock and scrap action. Expressly NOT decided here: the observation types themselves, which a person enters, and whether an observation type is tied to a scrap reason - the Scrap Reasons master records why scrap arose, not what a checker saw, and the two are not merged by this decision.
