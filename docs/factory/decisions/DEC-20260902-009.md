THE SAMPLE COUNT ON THE FINISHED-GOODS QUALITY SCREEN IS ENTERED BY THE CHECKER EACH TIME; THERE IS NO SAMPLE-SIZE RULE. On the Quality screen after a completed batch (DEC-20260902-006) the checker records how many pieces were actually sampled, and the ERP uses that figure as the divisor for the average measured weight per piece (DEC-20260902-007). No minimum, fixed count or per-product rule is enforced or suggested, because nobody has stated one and an agent may not invent it. The sample count is Quality evidence only: it does not move stock, book scrap, reject the batch or block the approval chain. A minimum sample size per product, if the owner ever wants one, is a NEW decision that adds a warning on top of this rule and changes nothing recorded here.
