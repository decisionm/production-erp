THE PERSON WHO PERFORMS THE FINISHED-GOODS QUALITY CHECK CANNOT APPROVE THE SAME BATCH AS PLANT MANAGER. This adds the third four-eyes comparison to the approval chain (FC-05): the checker must differ from the person who completed the batch (already enforced), the Plant Manager approver must differ from the checker (this decision), and the Accounts signer must differ from the Plant Manager (already enforced). The existing allow-same-user flag (production.approvals.allow_same_user) may relax this comparison ONLY when it is explicitly enabled for a one-person operation, in the open and for everyone, exactly as it relaxes the other two; the relaxation must NEVER happen automatically because a person holds both the Quality and the Plant Manager roles, and there is no Administrator exemption. The system must record who performed both actions - the checker and the Plant Manager approver - on the batch, whether or not the flag is on. Quality evidence and the OK/rejected count are unchanged (DEC-20260902-006).
