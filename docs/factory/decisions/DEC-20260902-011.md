THE INCOMING QUALITY SCREEN IS ONE SCREEN PER GRN LINE, AND ITS CHECKLIST IS EVIDENCE BESIDE THE ACCEPTED AND REJECTED QUANTITIES. It shows, read-only: the GRN and PO reference, the received date, the material, the received quantity and unit, the lot number, and for weighed materials the bag count, the bag barcodes and the weights already recorded at receipt. It does NOT show supplier identity or purchase rate to Quality: FC-06 stands as written, and the PO and GRN references provide enough traceability without them. The inspector records: the number of bags sampled (weighed materials only), the measured sample weight beside the receipt weights, visual observations ticked from a maintained list entered by an authorised person (as DEC-20260902-008 provides for finished goods), one optional note, and the accepted and rejected quantities. The inspected quantity IS the full received quantity and is never typed again; accepted plus rejected must equal received. The checklist changes nothing in stock: the accepted and rejected quantities remain the only stock action, and no second stock path is created. For counted packaging materials the bag and weight fields are hidden until Q87 defines how a counted arrival is recorded. EXPRESSLY NOT DECIDED HERE: whether QC may split one bag's kilograms, what Tally receives for a rejection, and any tolerance or verdict on the sample weight.
