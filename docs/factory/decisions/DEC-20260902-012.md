INCOMING QUALITY ACCEPTS OR REJECTS WHOLE BAGS ONLY; NO BAG IS EVER SPLIT AND NO BAG REMAINS ON HOLD AFTER INSPECTION. On the incoming Quality screen (DEC-20260902-011) the inspector SELECTS the rejected bag barcodes. The ERP calculates the rejected kilograms from the selected bags and the remaining quantity of the line as accepted. The inspector must not type a rejected quantity, and the screen must not accept a figure that would split a bag. After inspection every bag of the line is either accepted and issuable or rejected; no bag stays on hold because of a partial rejection, which retires the earlier behaviour of leaving a bag the rejection ended inside on hold with a note. A bag's identity is the unit of acceptance because it is the unit the barcode carries (DEC-20260831-010). For a counted material that carries no bags, the typed accepted and rejected quantities stand until Q87 defines how a counted arrival is recorded. Expressly NOT decided here: what becomes of any bag the earlier behaviour already left on hold on the live instance - that is counted on live before the build, never assumed - and what Tally receives for a rejection.
