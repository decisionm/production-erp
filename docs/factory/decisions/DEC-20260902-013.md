A COUNTED ARRIVAL WAITS FOR INCOMING QUALITY BEFORE THE STORE MAY ISSUE IT, EXACTLY AS A WEIGHED ONE DOES. For a material received by count - cartons, trays, pouches and every other item not measured in kilograms - the whole received quantity of the GRN line is HELD from every outflow door until the incoming Quality screen (DEC-20260902-011) records its accepted and rejected pieces. The hold is on the LINE QUANTITY and needs no bags: it does not wait for Q87's unit or barcode answer, and it is enforced at the balance the way the weighed-material hold already is (DEC-20260825-001), outranking every issue, transfer, store issue, completion and delivery. Accepted pieces become issuable; rejected pieces leave stock through the same Rejections Out issue a weighed rejection uses, because the accepted and rejected quantities are the only stock action (DEC-20260902-011). This makes DEC-20260831-011 - every arrival waits for incoming QA - true for every material, and retires the limit under which a counted arrival was issuable the moment its GRN was saved. Resolves the second part of Q87 (does a counted arrival wait for QA). Expressly NOT decided here: what ONE unit of a counted arrival is and what carries its barcode (the first and third parts of Q87), and what Tally receives for a rejection.
