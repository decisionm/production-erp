A COUNTED ARRIVAL IS RECORDED AS ITS HANDLING UNITS, ONE BARCODE PER UNIT, NEVER ONE LABEL PER PIECE. For counted packaging materials one unit is the physical handling unit in which the supplier delivered it - a bundle, bale, pallet, roll or box. The GRN records the number of handling units, the pieces contained in each unit, and one barcode for each handling unit; the total pieces across all units must equal the GRN's received quantity. Incoming Quality accepts or rejects whole labelled units, selecting them exactly as DEC-20260902-012 selects bags, and the quantity hold of DEC-20260902-013 releases unit by unit. The Store scans the unit barcode when issuing the material; if only part of an accepted unit is issued, the same barcode remains active with its reduced piece balance. No label is created per individual carton, tray or pouch. The label must not show supplier identity or purchase rate (FC-06).
