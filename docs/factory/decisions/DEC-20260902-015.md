THE ERP POSTS NO TALLY VOUCHER FOR AN INCOMING-QUALITY REJECTION. Nothing is posted or staged to Tally when incoming Quality rejects material: the ERP records the rejected quantity and the Rejections Out reference (the stock issue DEC-20260902-012 computes from the rejected bags, or DEC-20260902-013 from rejected pieces), and both must be VISIBLE on the Supplier Bill screen against the matching GRN line so that Accounts can match the supplier's paper debit note or credit note to them. Accounts continues to make the required Tally entry manually from the actual supplier document, as it does today. This closes the clause that DEC-20260825-001, DEC-20260830-001, DEC-20260901-003 and DEC-20260901-006 each left open for incoming rejection. It does NOT answer Q68 and does not authorise any supplier-bill or purchase posting from the ERP; if Q68 later introduces an approved purchase-posting flow, this rejection handling is reviewed with Accounts then. Expressly NOT decided here: whether the ERP should refuse a supplier-bill quantity above the Quality-accepted quantity - the bill remains a record of the supplier's paper, which may carry the full quantity with the rejection credited separately, and no such limit exists or is introduced.
