OF THE TEN WORKBOOK FACTORY RULES, EXACTLY ONE IS ENFORCED: A REASON IS REQUIRED WHENEVER THE SUPERVISOR OVERRIDES THE CONFIGURED CYCLE TIME OR CAVITY VALUE AT START BATCH. The override must still remain within the machine and product-configuration limits: a value outside those limits remains REFUSED, and entering a reason never bypasses a safety or configuration limit. The reason, the original value, the selected value and the person are recorded in the batch snapshot and the audit trail. The other nine Factory Rules - the global cycle-time minimum and maximum, default shift hours, the two material-variance thresholds, the two override permissions and the two downtime-timing allowances - remain reference values, clearly marked 'Not in use', read by no rule; the cycle-time bounds the floor enforces stay the per-machine and per-configuration ones, and the override permissions stay gated as they are. The variance thresholds are the subject of a separate decision and are not decided here. Enforcing any further row requires a new decision. Resolves Q93.
