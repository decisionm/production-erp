CONSUMPTION VARIANCE REMAINS ADVISORY ONLY; THE ERP NEVER REFUSES A COMPLETION OR AN APPROVAL ON IT. The expected consumption, the actual consumption, the variance percentage and the unaccounted kilograms are shown clearly at Complete Batch, at the Quality review, at Plant Manager approval and at Accounts approval. The Plant Manager and Accounts must see these figures before they sign, and the ERP must not automatically refuse completion or approval because of them. Both blocking-threshold settings (production tolerance variance_blocking_pct and unaccounted_blocking_kg) stay DISABLED. No figure is copied from the workbook's reference rows, which stay Not in use under DEC-20260902-021. If the factory later approves specific percentage and kilogram thresholds, they are introduced through a NEW owner decision, entered by a person, never invented. The completion's consumption rules are otherwise unchanged (DEC-20260901-007, DEC-20260902-019).
