PURCHASE REQUISITION AND PURCHASE ORDER PICKERS SHOW RAW MATERIAL AND PACKING MATERIAL BY DEFAULT; OTHER PURCHASABLE ITEMS SIT BEHIND A DELIBERATE CHOICE. A 'Show additional purchasable items' choice may also show consumables, spares or tooling, and unclassified items - the last with a clear warning. An unclassified item requires a reason and an authorised person on the document until its category is corrected by a person (DEC-20260827-002: NULL means not recorded yet). Finished goods, and any produced or work-in-progress product, must NEVER appear in either picker. Every additional item purchased through this path follows the normal workflow without exception - requisition approval, purchase order, goods receipt, incoming Quality (DEC-20260902-013 for a counted arrival) and stock recording - and must not bypass any of those controls. This keeps the normal picker clean while letting materials such as mould-release spray and genuine spares be purchased and stocked, so that a material a completion may consume (DEC-20260902-019) cannot drift negative for want of a purchase path. Resolves Q59(a), and Q59(d) for purchase documents only. Expressly NOT decided here: Q59(b) store material requests and Q59(c) sales orders.
