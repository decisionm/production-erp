ANY USER WITH THE PROCUREMENT WRITE PERMISSION MAY APPROVE A PURCHASE REQUISITION, EXCEPT THE PERSON WHO RAISED IT; THIS RULE APPLIES TO APPROVAL ONLY. Self-approval is REFUSED with a clear message. The system records the requester, the approver and the approval time on the requisition. Administrator status must not silently bypass this rule: there is no Administrator exemption, exactly as the batch approval chain has none (FC-05). The Store may raise a requisition, but the same Store user cannot approve their own requisition. No new role or permission is created; if the factory later wants approval restricted to a particular role, that is introduced through a separate decision. REJECTION remains an approver action and carries NO requester comparison from this decision. A requester may WITHDRAW or CANCEL their own requisition through a separate action of their own, which is not a rejection. This supersedes DEC-20260902-024 solely to withdraw its last sentence - the agent's inferred clause that rejection follows the same four-eyes comparison as approval - which the owner has withdrawn; every other word of that record stands unchanged here.
