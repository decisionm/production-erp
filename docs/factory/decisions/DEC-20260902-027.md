THE PRODUCTION-INPUT FLAG REMAINS THE ONLY ELIGIBILITY RULE FOR STORE MATERIAL REQUESTS AND STORE ISSUES; CATEGORY IS INFORMATIONAL. An active item is requestable only when an authorised person has marked it as a production input, regardless of its current category. Category must not automatically enable or refuse an item on a material request or a Store Issue. If the flag conflicts with the category - for example a finished good marked as a production input - the screen shows a clear warning so the master data can be reviewed, but the flag is never changed silently. The ERP records who changed the production-input flag and when. Consumables and currently unclassified materials such as mould-release spray may remain requestable when their flag is deliberately enabled. Resolves Q59(b) as a confirmation of the shipped flag rule; Q59(c) sales orders stays open.
