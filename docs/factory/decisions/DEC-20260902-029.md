A STORE USER RE-POINTS A FINISHED-GOODS HOLD ON THEIR OWN JUDGEMENT; NO APPROVAL IS REQUIRED. When the Store moves a hold from one sales-order line to another (DEC-20260902-028), the re-point takes effect at once, in one step that releases the old hold and places the new one, with the original line, the new line, the quantity, the reason and the person recorded and the action audited. No Sales, Owner or other approval stands between the Store's decision and the stock, because the Store performs the dispatch (DEC-20260901-005) and the reason and the person are on record. This confirms the shipped behaviour as the factory's rule. An approval step for re-points, proposed in a Codex review and considered, was NOT adopted; introducing one later would be a new decision. Resolves Q62(b).
