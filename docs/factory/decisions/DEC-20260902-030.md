THERE IS NO PERMANENT CUSTOMER PRIORITY. Every sales order is treated equally by the ERP. When available finished-goods stock cannot cover competing orders, the Store decides the re-pointing case by case and records the reason (DEC-20260902-028, DEC-20260902-029). The ERP must not automatically rank customers, move holds, or infer priority from order value, customer name, promised date or previous sales. A permanent priority may be introduced later only through a new owner decision naming the relevant customers. Resolves Q62(c).
