A QUEUED PRODUCTION REQUEST IS ANSWERED WHEN ITS SALES LINE IS COVERED, WHERE COVERED MEANS DELIVERED PLUS HELD; A STARTED JOB IS NEVER RETIRED BY PAPERWORK. A sales-order line is covered when the pieces already delivered against it plus the pieces still held for it reach the line's quantity, counted so that a delivered hold is never counted twice. A request still QUEUED leaves the floor's queue the moment its line is covered. A request the floor has already STARTED is never retired automatically: whether a running job stops is the floor's call, made by a person. This confirms the shipped formula as the factory's rule, and is the only reading that never marks a line produced on a hold that is later re-pointed (DEC-20260902-028, DEC-20260902-029). Resolves Q62(e).
