A SALES ORDER MAY CONTAIN ACTIVE FINISHED GOOD ITEMS ONLY. The sales-order picker shows only active items whose confirmed item-master category is Finished Good, and the server refuses creating or amending a sales order with any other category even if the screen is bypassed. Sellability is never inferred from an item's name or Tally group at transaction time; only the confirmed item-master category (DEC-20260827-001) is used. Scrap, raw material, packing material, consumables, spares, tooling, work in progress, Other and unclassified items cannot be added to a new sales order. Existing historical sales orders remain readable and unchanged. If the factory genuinely starts selling one of those items later, its category and sales eligibility require a new owner decision, as DEC-20260827-001 already says for scrap. Together with DEC-20260902-023 and DEC-20260902-027 this resolves Q59 in full.
