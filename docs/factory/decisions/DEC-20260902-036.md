A DAMAGED PET RESIN BAG MAY GO TO QUALITY HOLD ONLY WHEN IT HAS NOT BEEN TIPPED INTO THE BIN AND REMAINS PHYSICALLY IDENTIFIABLE BY ITS BARCODE. This is an EXPLICIT AND NARROW NARROWING of DEC-20260902-003 ('material that goes into the common day bin is never returned to the Store') and of DEC-20260902-004 ('PET resin, once issued, is never returned to the Store'), declared here and not an interpretation: both records stand for every other case. The exception works as follows. It never returns directly to usable Store stock. The damaged bag is scanned at the return and its actual remaining kilograms are recorded; that quantity moves from Production/WIP to Quality hold (the internal location DEC-20260901-003 names), and the same quantity is removed from the resin pool in the same transaction so it is never later counted as consumption. Quality then either confirms damage to scrap or releases the material through the existing Quality path (DEC-20260901-003). Resin already tipped into the bin, and any material with no identifiable container or barcode, cannot use this path. The scan of a damaged bag at the return is a return scan, not a load scan, and is the one exception to the no-second-scan rule of DEC-20260902-002. The return is the Store's act as DEC-20260831-005 provides; who physically holds the scanner at that moment is not decided here. Expressly NOT decided here: what Tally receives when the damaged resin is scrapped, which DEC-20260901-003 already leaves open.
