THE HANDLING-UNIT BLOCK IS MANDATORY ON EVERY COUNTED GRN LINE; A COUNTED ARRIVAL WITH NO UNITS IS REFUSED. For a material received by count, the goods receipt must record its handling units - the number of units, the pieces in each and one barcode per unit (DEC-20260902-014) - exactly as a weighed arrival must carry its bags (DEC-20260831-010). The Store names the unit as the supplier delivered it: a pallet of loose cartons is one unit, a bundle is one unit, a roll is one unit, and the Store labels it at the gate. A counted line offered with no units is refused, and the refusal names what is missing; nothing invents a unit. This is a declared narrowing of DEC-20260902-013: the hold on a counted line stays on the line quantity, and from the day this rule is switched on it is released unit by unit; the typed accepted-and-rejected path of -013 remains only for counted arrivals received BEFORE the handling-unit build, which keep it until they are consumed. ROLLOUT, part of the rule as for DEC-20260902-017 and -018: the refusal is switched on only after the live packaging item masters have been checked by a person and the Store can label units at the gate; until then a counted line is received by quantity and held under -013. Expressly NOT decided here: what Tally receives for a rejected unit (open as before).
