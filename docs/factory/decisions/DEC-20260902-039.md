THERE IS ONE PRODUCTION/WIP PET RESIN FIGURE, WITH A RULE FOR EACH OF ITS USES; NO SECOND DAY-BIN FIGURE OR BALANCE IS EVER CREATED. Every screen labels the PET resin Production/WIP balance 'Estimated', in a short label and never a paragraph, and it is never presented as a physically counted day-bin quantity (DEC-20260807-007 unchanged). The displayed value stays negative when it is negative, with a discrepancy warning; only material-request netting treats a negative value as zero, and netting uses the positive estimate as the quantity already available in Production/WIP exactly as DEC-20260831-005 provides. Other materials' balances remain ordinary stock-ledger figures and carry no estimate label. Tally reconciliation compares each item's total across the factory's one operational godown (DEC-20260830-002, DEC-20260806-009); it must never treat the PET resin Production/WIP row as a separately counted Tally balance and must never re-anchor it automatically, because the bin is never weighed. Any Tally difference remains visible for review and must not silently rewrite the estimated bin balance. This states which treatment governs where for DEC-20260902-005 and changes none of the three earlier decisions.
