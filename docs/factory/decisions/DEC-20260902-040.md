DEC-20260902-029 AND DEC-20260902-031 BOTH STAND, CONFIRMED IN THE OWNER'S OWN WORDS AFTER A REVIEW ASKED WHOSE WORDS THEY RESTED ON. Nothing is changed by this record; it exists so that neither rule rests on the owner endorsing an agent's pick. For DEC-20260902-029: the Store may re-point a finished-goods hold from one sales-order line to another without a separate approval; the Store records the reason, the person, the quantity, the original line and the new line; the action is audited; the ERP never moves a hold automatically. For DEC-20260902-031: pending production requests are sorted by the promised date, earliest first; undated requests come after dated ones; equal dates follow the time raised; Production may manually move a request with a recorded reason, and that manual position stays until Production removes it; started or completed jobs are never reordered; the production queue order does not create customer priority and never moves or re-points a stock hold. Both records remain the governing text; this confirmation adds no rule.
