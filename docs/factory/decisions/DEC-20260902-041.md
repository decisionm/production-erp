QUALITY APPROVES A SALES ORDER LINE ONLY AFTER ITS FULL QUANTITY IS HELD; THE STORE MAY THEN DISPATCH THE APPROVED QUANTITY IN PARTS, AND THE APPROVAL CANNOT BE WITHDRAWN ONCE ANY OF IT HAS GONE. Internal Quality records dispatch approval for a line only when the full line quantity is held (DEC-20260831-006 unchanged). After that approval the Store (DEC-20260901-005) may dispatch the approved quantity in one or more partial deliveries: each dispatch reduces ERP stock only by the quantity actually dispatched; the line remains partly dispatched until the whole approved quantity has gone; the total dispatched must never exceed the approved quantity, and the Store can never dispatch more than the remaining approved quantity. After the first dispatch the Quality approval cannot be withdrawn, and the remaining approved quantity may be dispatched later under the same approval. Sales is never required to split a line to dispatch in parts, and no truck needs a fresh Quality approval. This is a DECLARED clarification of DEC-20260831-006: it closes that record's expressly open clause on whether Quality may withdraw an approval after goods have moved (no, once any quantity has been dispatched), and states the partial-dispatch reading of 'a line may not be dispatched beyond the quantity Quality approved'. Whether an approval may be withdrawn BEFORE any dispatch is not decided here.
