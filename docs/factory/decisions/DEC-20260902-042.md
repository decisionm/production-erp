COMPLETED FINISHED GOODS ARE QUALITY-PENDING UNTIL THE BATCH'S QUALITY CHECK RECORDS THEM; ONLY THE OK QUANTITY BECOMES AVAILABLE AND HOLDABLE. Finished goods received into the Store at Complete Batch (DEC-20260902-016) may be visible in the Store as Quality Pending, but they must not be available for holding, dispatch approval or delivery until the batch's Quality stage (DEC-20260902-006) records the OK and rejected counts. After that, only the OK quantity becomes available and holdable; the rejected quantity follows the scrap process (FC-02, DEC-20260805-001). A hold can therefore only ever cover checked goods, and Internal Quality's line-level dispatch approval (DEC-20260831-006, DEC-20260902-041) is unchanged. This is enforced ON THE SERVER for both the carton-scan and the typed delivery paths: a hold, an approval or a delivery that would draw on Quality-pending goods is refused, never merely hidden. The mechanism is the same by-construction one incoming QA and the damaged-return hold already use - a stock state the Store cannot issue from (DEC-20260825-001, DEC-20260901-003) - and the Store dashboard's completed, Quality-pending, approved and rejected figures (DEC-20260902-016) read from it. ROLLOUT, part of the rule: before this is switched on, a READ-ONLY count of the existing live Quality-pending finished goods is taken on the live instance and reviewed by a person; nothing is assumed and no stock is moved by the rollout. Expressly NOT decided here: which internal location or state name carries the Quality-pending goods, an engineering choice, and what happens to any live goods found pending at rollout, which the count informs and a person decides.
