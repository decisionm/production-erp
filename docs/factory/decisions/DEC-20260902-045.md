AMENDING A CONFIRMED SALES ORDER: SALES MAY CHANGE THE DATE OR REDUCE AN UNDISPATCHED LINE WITH A MANDATORY REASON; AN INCREASE IS A NEW LINE; AFTER ANY DISPATCH A LINE CANNOT BE CANCELLED, ONLY CLOSED SHORT. A reduction cannot go below the quantity already dispatched; it must release the excess holds and cancel only production requests that have NOT started - a started production job is never stopped automatically (DEC-20260902-032). An increase in quantity creates a new line, which is held, approved and dispatched on its own. After any dispatch the line cannot be cancelled; it may only be closed short with a reason. Every cancellation - of an order or a line - records the person, the date and a mandatory reason. A date change flags the request as moved in the production queue (DEC-20260902-031). Resolves the cancellation-reason part of Q44.
