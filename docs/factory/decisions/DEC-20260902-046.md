A TALLY SALES INVOICE MATCHES ONLY A CONFIRMED ERP ORDER, AND ACCOUNTS - NOT SALES - IMPORTS AND RESOLVES UNMATCHED INVOICES. A Tally invoice may match only a confirmed ERP sales order; a match that would land on a draft remains unmatched with that reason recorded. Accounts runs the Tally Sales Invoice import and resolves unmatched invoices: Accounts may link one to an existing confirmed order for the same customer and the same customer PO reference, or mark it 'No ERP order' with a reason. The ERP never creates a sales order automatically from an imported invoice (DEC-20260831-012 unchanged). Sales may see the resulting match status on the order. Resolves the draft-invoice part of Q44.
