CARTON SCAN IS THE FINAL REQUIRED DISPATCH METHOD; TYPED DISPATCH IS ALLOWED ONLY FOR IDENTIFIED LEGACY STOCK WITH NO CARTON IDENTITY, BY AN AUTHORISED PERSON WITH A REASON. Carton-tracked stock must always be scanned at dispatch. Typed dispatch is permitted only for stock identified as legacy - finished goods that carry no carton identity - and each typed dispatch records the authorised person and the reason. The ERP must not switch an entire product to scan-only automatically merely because one batch carries cartons: the rule attaches to the stock, not to the product. ROLLOUT, part of the rule: the legacy stock on the live instance is counted, read-only, before this is switched on. Dispatch approval and stock effects are unchanged (DEC-20260831-006, DEC-20260902-041, DEC-20260902-042).
