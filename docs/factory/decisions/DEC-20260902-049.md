A DISPATCH IS APPEND-ONLY, AND A WRONG DISPATCH IS CORRECTED BY AN AUDITED DISPATCH-REVERSAL DOCUMENT, NEVER BY EDITING OR DELETING THE ORIGINAL. A dispatch record is never edited or deleted. A wrong dispatch is corrected by a separate dispatch-reversal document that records the person and the reason and reverses the stock effect, leaving the original dispatch intact and both visible. An actual customer return is a separate workflow and goes through Quality; it is not a reversal and is not decided here.
