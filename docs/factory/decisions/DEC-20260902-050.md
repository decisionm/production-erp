THE TALLY LEDGER IMPORT IS THE CUSTOMER-MASTER SOURCE; A CUSTOMER CREATED BY SALES STAYS PENDING UNTIL ACCOUNTS MAPS IT TO A TALLY LEDGER. Customers come from the Tally ledger import, run on the live instance dry-run first (the manual workflow, write only after the dry run is read). A new customer created by Sales by hand remains PENDING - usable for nothing that needs a ledger - until Accounts maps it to a Tally ledger. A GSTIN is mandatory only for a GST-registered customer, not for every customer. FC-06 is unchanged: Sales sees derived money and never the bags behind it.
