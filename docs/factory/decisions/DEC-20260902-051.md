A SALES ROLE IS DEFINED WITH SALES READ AND WRITE ONLY, PLUS THE SCOPED STOCK-AVAILABILITY AND PRODUCTION-STATUS VIEWS SALES NEEDS. The Sales role holds sales.view and sales.manage. It is given only the scoped views of stock availability and production status that Sales needs to answer a customer - read-only and limited to finished goods and the order's own requests - and NOT broad inventory, production or finance permissions. Defined the way the Store role was: a role definition command run dry-run first, assigning nobody unless an existing active login is named.
