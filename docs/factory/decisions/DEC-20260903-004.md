THE ERP'S OWN SALES INVOICE IS RETIRED; THE IMPORTED TALLY SALES INVOICE, MATCHED TO THE ORDER, IS THE ERP'S INVOICE RECORD. Tally originates the sales invoice, the e-invoice and the IRN (DEC-20260831-012) and the ERP imports and matches it to a confirmed order (DEC-20260902-046), so the ERP raises no invoice document of its own: the Invoices screen and the routes that create or issue an ERP invoice are withdrawn, and no proforma document replaces them (quotations are out of scope, DEC-20260902-052). Existing ERP invoice rows stay as read-only history, never edited or deleted, and an order's trace may still show them as history. Figures the ERP currently builds on its own invoices - receivables, GST returns - are no longer built on the retired document; where they read from instead is answered by the Tally invoice import build (DEC-20260902-046), not by this record. Until the routes are withdrawn, an ERP invoice can never bill more than the customer ordered (PR #88). Resolves Q96.
