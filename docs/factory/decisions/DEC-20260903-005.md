A day of attendance is judged by the HOURS WORKED against the eight-hour factory shift, not by the Full Day or Half Day label printed by the Pooja punch app. Eight hours is a full day and four hours is a half day. The app label cannot be trusted: in the July 2026 report 245 days were marked Half Day and 232 of those had seven hours or more worked, because the app measures every shift against a longer general day, so real eight-hour shifts that clocked out a few minutes early were recorded as halves. Applying that month as printed would have paid 232 full shifts as halves, about 116 days of pay across 59 people in one month. The factory runs three shifts, roughly 06:00 to 14:00, 14:00 to 22:00 and 22:00 to 06:00, plus a 09:00 to 18:00 office day, which is why one yardstick cannot judge them all.
