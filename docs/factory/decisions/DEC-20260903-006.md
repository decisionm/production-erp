THE ACCOUNTS ROLE HOLDS FULL PROCUREMENT WRITE, SO ACCOUNTS MAY APPROVE A PURCHASE REQUISITION RAISED BY SOMEONE ELSE. The owner's instruction is that the Store, the Administrator and Accounts hold the right to approve requisitions. Approval is gated on the procurement write permission (DEC-20260902-025), and the procurement module has ONE write permission covering requisitions and their approval, purchase orders, goods receipts and vendors; there is no narrower approve-only grant. The owner was told this and chose full procurement for Accounts, the same trade-off already accepted for the Store role in its own decision of 02-Sep-2026 (on the open Store-role pull request). Accounts therefore holds procurement.view and procurement.manage. FC-06 is unchanged: Accounts already holds finance and sees rates by right. The grant is made in PermissionSeeder with givePermissionTo and never syncPermissions, following the carton-trace and added-line precedents (DEC-20260901-007), so a deploy only ever ADDS this permission and never rewrites what an Administrator configured on the Roles screen. On the live instance the same grant is made at once by an Administrator on the Roles screen, which the seeder later confirms rather than replaces. The requester rule stands: a requisition is never approved by the person who raised it, whatever role they hold.
