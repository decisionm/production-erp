A BATCH THAT HAS BEEN HANDED OVER MAY STILL BE RETURNED BY QUALITY AND CORRECTED BY THE SUPERVISOR; ONLY A CHANGE TO THE CLOSING COUNT THE NEXT SHIFT OPENED FROM IS REFUSED. The floor's flow is: start the batch, complete it, and it stands in the shift floor queue with its status; corrections are possible while it waits; Quality either records its check and the batch goes on to the Plant Manager and Accounts, or Quality sends it back; a batch sent back is corrected IN THE SHIFT FLOOR QUEUE. Nothing is ever sent back to a machine and no run is repeated - the machine has moved on and only the paperwork is wrong. Until now the ERP refused every correction to a segment that had handed over to a following one, on the ground that the following segment opens from this one's closing figures. That guard is WIDER THAN ITS OWN REASON: what a following segment inherits is exactly ONE number, the counted closing bin weight in kilograms, recorded as its own count movement - not the pieces produced, not the rejects, not the reason. So a batch with a following segment may be returned and corrected, and the refusal fires ONLY where the correction would change that closing count while a following segment stands on it; the refusal names that reason. THE SUPERVISOR may make the correction, not only the Plant Manager. EXPRESSLY NOT BUILT: correcting a chain - reopening the following segment and restating its opening - because that rewrites a shift somebody has already signed; where a correction genuinely needs the closing count changed, the ERP refuses today and the factory decides later whether a chain correction should exist. Every other correction gate stands unchanged: an approved, synced, failed or rejected batch, or one already on a Tally voucher, is still refused.
