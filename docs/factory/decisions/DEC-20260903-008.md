THE STORE ROLE HOLDS FULL PROCUREMENT WRITE, SO A STOREKEEPER MAY APPROVE A PURCHASE REQUISITION. Store already held procurement.view on the live instance and could see procurement but not act in it; approval is gated on the procurement WRITE permission, so the store user was refused. The whole change is one permission, procurement.manage, ADDED to the role - never syncing or rewriting the five other permissions an administrator granted Store through the Roles screen. THE TRADE-OFF, NAMED: the procurement module has ONE write permission covering requisitions and their approval, purchase orders, goods receipts and vendors; there is no narrower approve-only grant, so this also lets Store raise requisitions and purchase orders and record goods receipts - the same trade-off the owner accepted for Accounts in DEC-20260903-006. FC-06 IS INTACT AND HAS ONE PRACTICAL CONSEQUENCE. The purchase rate is Owner/Accounts only, and the gate ships: PurchaseOrderLineResource and GoodsReceiptNoteLineResource OMIT unit_price for a reader without finance.view or finance.manage, and supplier bills sit behind the finance module entirely, so Store cannot READ a purchase rate. But creating and amending a purchase order both REQUIRE unit_price, so a Store user cannot amend a purchase order without typing a rate they cannot see, which would overwrite the real one. PURCHASE ORDERS THEREFORE STAY WITH ACCOUNTS AND THE OWNER IN PRACTICE; what this grant is for is requisition approval and goods receipts.
